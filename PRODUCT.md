# Product

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## What it is
Framerald is a personal studio for making images and short videos with Google's models on Vertex AI. One owner, no sign-in. It is a tool someone sits in for hours: write a prompt, get frames back, keep the good ones, repeat.

## Users
One person, working alone at a desk, usually in a dim room, comparing many generated images side by side. Cares about the pictures, not the chrome.

## Tone
Quiet, dark and precise, in the way a film grading suite is. The work is the hero. Plain words. No hype.

## Design system
Cinematic Dark tokens with an emerald brand accent and one rainbow border beam on the prompt box. Fixed; see docs/ai-context/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.

## Anti-references
Generic "AI tool" look: sparkle icons, purple gradients, glow everywhere, stat-card rows, marketing language inside the app.

## Principles
1. Scan first: results are large, controls are dense and quiet.
2. One bright thing per screen.
3. Every state is drawn: loading, empty, error, full.
4. Show the cost of what was made.
